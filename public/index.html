Talk to a Googler

A live practice call. They speak, you answer out loud, they follow up — the reply starts within a couple of seconds and you can cut in whenever you like. At the end you get a read on how you came across: clarity, concision, structure, specificity, warmth.

Watch an interview

Two agents run the interview while you watch — one being interviewed, one asking. The point is the communication: how a strong candidate opens, what they check before writing anything, when they volunteer complexity without being asked.

Resume questions

Upload your resume and get the questions an interviewer would actually ask about it — role by role, project by project, plus the uncomfortable ones about gaps and unquantified claims.

Your file is not stored. It is read in memory to extract the text, sent once to the model to generate questions and a short candidate profile, then discarded. Nothing is written to disk and no resume content is logged. The profile is kept in your account (synced across your devices), and goes along with interview requests while it is switched on.
